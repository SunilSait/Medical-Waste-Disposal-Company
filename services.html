Sharps Disposal

Sharps Waste Collection & Destruction

Our sharps disposal program provides complete management of needles, syringes, scalpels, lancets, and other sharp medical instruments. We supply FDA-cleared, puncture-resistant containers in multiple sizes and manage the entire lifecycle from placement through certified destruction.

Each container is barcoded for full chain-of-custody tracking, and every destruction event generates an auditable Certificate of Destruction delivered directly to your compliance dashboard.

Barcoded chain-of-custody tracking
Multiple container sizes (1-quart to 18-gallon)
Automated replacement upon pickup

Biohazard Waste

Infectious & Biohazardous Waste Treatment

Our biohazard waste program handles the most critical category of medical waste with the highest level of safety and regulatory compliance. We manage blood and body fluids, contaminated dressings, surgical waste, microbiological cultures, and any materials that may carry infectious agents.

Treatment methods include steam autoclaving at 134°C for complete pathogen destruction, with real-time temperature and pressure monitoring to ensure sterility assurance levels meet or exceed regulatory requirements.

Pharmaceutical Waste

Pharmaceutical & Chemotherapy Waste Disposal

Our pharmaceutical waste program covers every class of medication waste, from expired over-the-counter products to hazardous chemotherapy agents and DEA-controlled substances. We provide color-coded containers, staff training on proper segregation, and full DEA manifest documentation.

All pharmaceutical waste undergoes high-temperature incineration at EPA-permitted facilities, with complete documentation of the destruction process for regulatory compliance and audit readiness.

DEA-compliant controlled substance disposal
Color-coded segregation containers
High-temperature incineration
Chemotherapy waste handling (P/U-listed)
Full manifest & DEA Form 41 support
