Our Mission

Making Healthcare Safer Through Responsible Disposal

Founded in 2009 by a team of environmental health professionals, SafeDispose was built on the belief that medical waste management should never compromise the safety of healthcare workers, communities, or the environment.

Today, we serve over 2,500 healthcare facilities across the nation — from single-physician practices to multi-campus hospital systems — delivering compliant, transparent, and sustainable waste disposal solutions backed by industry-leading technology.

Our Values

What Drives Us Every Day

Our core values shape every decision, from the technology we invest in to the way we serve our clients.

The safety of healthcare workers, communities, and our own team is non-negotiable. Every protocol is designed with zero-incident operations as the baseline.
